A simple beginning

From download
to your first conversation.

  1. Move it into place.

    Open the DMG and drag Athena into Applications.

  2. Sign in, then speak naturally.

    Use your enabled Athena account. New accounts have no credit; payments are not open.

  3. Keep your own space.

    App conversations and files stay on your Mac. The web version runs on our Linux server; histories do not yet sync between them. To uninstall, quit Athena and move the app to the Trash. Your files remain.
