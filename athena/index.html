Choose where the work happens

In your browser

Open Athena without installing an app. Conversations and files live on our server. This version cannot control your own computer; hosted shell and browser execution are not enabled in this preview.

On your Mac

The app runs its assistant workspace on your Mac, with local files and conversations. Managed browsing uses an installed Chrome or Chromium in a separate profile. Model requests use your Athena account balance.

An early preview, with clear boundaries.

Access is limited to enabled accounts. New accounts start with no credit and there is no recharge service yet. Mac and web histories do not sync yet. Full desktop control, real-time voice calling and other native platforms are still in development.
