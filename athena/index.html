Choose where the work happens

In your browser

The web version runs on our Linux server. Its conversations, files and execution environment are separate from your Mac. Opening the website does not give it access to your computer.

On your Mac

Athena works on your Mac, keeping its conversations and files locally. The hosted preview uses an enabled Athena account for model access. Local setup with your own provider key is being prepared for the open-source release.

An early preview, with clear boundaries.

This is an early preview. Hosted access requires an enabled account with credit. Mac and web histories do not sync. Voice and computer interactions are evolving and may require setup and system permissions. Other platform downloads are not yet published.
