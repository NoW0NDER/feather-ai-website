The idea behind Athena

Thoughtful by default.
Yours by degrees.

I.

Less to learn.

Use your own words. You shouldn’t need to know which model, tool or setting will get you there.

II.

Room to make it yours.

A useful table, a small tool, a clearer way to see a result. We’re building a desktop assistant whose work surfaces can change with the task.

III.

A clear way back.

See what is happening. Stop when you need to. Restore the default experience without losing your conversations and files.
