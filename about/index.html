The idea behind Feather

Make intelligence
feel within reach.

Useful technology leaves you more attention for your own life. We are building Athena around that simple ambition.

Three things we return to

01

Start with everyday language.

Start in your own words, without learning commands. Hosted access handles model configuration for you. The forthcoming local mode is for people who want to choose their own provider and key.

02

Change the interface when it helps.

A table can be better than a paragraph. A focused workspace can be better than a long conversation. Adaptation should serve the work, without becoming work itself.

03

Keep a clear way back.

Progress, history, stop and reset are part of the foundation. Changes to the workspace should not take away the ability to recover.

Know where your work goes

The Mac app stores conversations and files on your Mac; the web version stores them on our server. Content needed for a request is sent to the configured model service. Hosted access uses our account gateway. The planned local mode will use your own provider credentials. Local storage does not mean offline AI.

Before you begin

Can anyone use it today?

Hosted access is currently limited to enabled accounts with credit. The open-source release and local bring-your-own-key setup are in preparation; they are not yet a published download.

Are my Mac and web conversations shared?

Not yet. They use the same account service, but their conversation and file stores are separate.

What happens when I reset or uninstall?

Reset disables custom workspace extensions and preserves chats and files. Removing the Mac app also leaves its data in your Library; deleting that data is a separate action.

What is available now?

An Apple Silicon Mac preview and an authenticated web version. The Mac test build is not Apple-notarized. Voice and computer interactions are being improved; see the download page for the available build and its requirements. Windows, iOS and Android downloads are not published.
