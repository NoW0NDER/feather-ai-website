The idea behind Feather

Make intelligence
feel within reach.

Useful technology leaves you more attention for your own life. We are building Athena around that simple ambition.

Three things we return to

01

Start with everyday language.

You should not have to learn model names, write commands or bring an API key. An enabled Athena account is the starting point.

02

Change the interface when it helps.

A table can be better than a paragraph. A focused workspace can be better than a long conversation. Adaptation should serve the work, without becoming work itself.

03

Keep a clear way back.

Progress, history, stop and reset are part of the foundation. Changes to the workspace should not take away the ability to recover.

Know where your work goes

The Mac app keeps its conversations and files on your Mac. The web version stores them on our server. Both send the content needed for your request to the configured model service through our account gateway. This is not an offline AI product. Credentials for that service are never bundled into the app.

Before you begin

Can anyone use it today?

You can create an account, but access currently requires operator enablement and a positive balance. New accounts have neither. Payments and recharge are not available.

Are my Mac and web conversations shared?

Not yet. They use the same account service, but their conversation and file stores are separate.

What happens when I reset or uninstall?

Reset disables custom workspace extensions and preserves chats and files. Removing the Mac app also leaves its data in your Library; deleting that data is a separate action.

What is available now?

An authenticated web preview and an Apple Silicon Mac test build. The Mac build has not been notarized by Apple. Voice calling, complete computer control, Windows, iOS and Android are not ready for release.
